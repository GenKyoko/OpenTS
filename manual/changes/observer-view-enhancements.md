---
title: Let a watcher read what a base is doing
category: feature
release: 0.2.0
targets: []
credit: [OpenTS contributors]
---

A player who is an observer now sees and can select cloaked and otherwise
invisible units, treating the whole map as sensed, so a clicked unit can be
picked up for viewing like any other. For the owner of a building, its allies
and the observer, a selected building shows the cameo of whatever its factory
is producing, a selected power plant shows its owner's power output and
drain in the wording the original game used, and a selected refinery or silo
shows its owner's money (the silo also shows how much tiberium it holds).
The old
spy-only production cameo and the selection-only, ally-only power readout are
superseded by these. Public settings and file formats are unchanged.
